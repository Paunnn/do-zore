# People

Built by `tools/art/people` (see its README) from:

- **MakeHuman** bodies, clothes and hair, through the MPFB 2 add-on for Blender
  (https://static.makehumancommunity.org). The base mesh, targets, rig weights and the asset packs used
  (makehuman_system_assets, faceunits01, hair01, shirts01, suits01, hats01, pants01, dress01) are
  released under CC0 1.0 (public domain); MPFB's code is GPL, which does not extend to what it makes.
- **Universal Animation Library 1 and 2** by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com), CC0 1.0: every clip
  in `people_anims.glb`.

The cartoon reshaping, the cleaned faces, the textures' cartoon treatment, the drawn faces
(`shaders/person.gdshader`), the hats, instruments and props are our own.
