# Research: how idle tycoon games present the venue, the map and the HUD

Reviewed from store screenshots (October 2026): Hotel Empire Tycoon, Idle Theme Park Tycoon and
Idle Burger Empire (Codigames), Idle Restaurant Tycoon (Kolibri), Nightclub Tycoon, Idle Food
Bar, The Idle Father, and My Cruise (earlier pass).

## What they do

**The map is the game world.** Hotel Empire and Theme Park never cut to a separate map screen
to show your business: the hotel is a roofless cut-away building on a real city lot, with
streets, parked cars, sidewalks, palms and the beach around it, and you pan across it. New
locations appear in place (an empty lot or a run-down building with a price) and become part of
the same world when bought. A small map button jumps between larger regions.

**Rendering.** All of the Codigames titles are lit low-poly 3D seen through an orthographic
isometric camera: soft directional light and shadows, warm interior lights, glowing windows,
saturated colours, simple chunky characters. Kolibri's 2D titles get a similar depth from
gradient lighting, soft contact shadows and much denser interior decoration (rugs, paintings,
plants, lamps, wall finishes). Flat vector art with uniform outlines, as we had, reads as
"drawing", not "place".

**HUD.** There is no frame around the world. Small dark translucent pills top-left carry the
currencies (icon + bold white number), a second pill or stars top-right carry rating/progress, a
column of round white icon buttons on the right opens the main screens (with red "!" badges when
something is affordable), and two or three large round action buttons sit at the bottom.
Timed boosts sit as badges on the left.

**Buttons and panels.** Glossy rounded buttons with a darker lip (green/red/yellow), white
labels with a dark outline; panels are white or cream cards with a coloured header ribbon and a
round close button; list rows have an icon, a title, a short line and a price button.

**Type.** Heavy rounded display lettering, white or gold fill, thick dark outline and a drop
shadow. Lilita One and Fredoka are the usual choices but lack č, ć and đ, so they are out for
Serbian. Candidates with full Serbian Latin coverage were rendered in the game treatment.

## Decisions for Do Zore

1. **One night city as the map.** The four venues sit in one continuous isometric city along
   the road the player travels: the birtija at the village edge, the kafana on a cobbled old-town
   street, the restoran on the central square, the splav moored on the river under a bridge. The
   active venue is a roofless cut-away full of guests; the next one shows its price on a sign;
   later ones are dark. Buying a venue flies the camera across the city to it.
2. **Real-time 3D in Godot** (Compatibility renderer, orthographic camera): moonlight with
   shadows, warm lamps and windows with glow, low-poly people built and animated in code. Static
   geometry is merged per material to keep draw calls low on phones; textures (wood, cloth,
   cobbles, kilims) are generated by `tools/art`.
3. **Floating HUD, kafana accents.** Money and mood pills top-left, settings and leaderboard
   top-right, a right-hand column of round buttons (map, band, drinks, upgrades) with badges,
   the music button as the main round action. Panels are cream cards with a red header ribbon
   and a checked-cloth trim; buttons are glossy with a lip.
4. **Type:** Shrikhand (retro sign-painter italic) for titles and venue names, Titan One for
   buttons and numbers, Nunito Black/ExtraBold for body text — all OFL, all cover Serbian Latin.
